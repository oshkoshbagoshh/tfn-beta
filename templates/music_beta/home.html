About Us

Three First Names & Associates — TFN Media

Company Overview

Three First Names & Associates combines a unique perspective on TV advertising with a passion for the music industry. We partner with brands and agencies to cut through the fragmented TV landscape and succeed with CTV—from linear-to-streaming strategy and campaign optimization to technical data integration and custom music for video ads.

What We Do

We offer TFN Media Solutions (CTV strategy, optimization, and vendor data integration) and TFN Music Services (custom music and a network of independent artists for your video and CTV campaigns). Based in Chicago, we work with brands across industries to make the most of streaming and creative.

Founded

Three First Names & Associates was founded in 2024. Headquarters: Chicago, Illinois.

About Nathan

Nathan Michael Scott is the founder of Three First Names & Associates (three first names—get it?). With nearly a decade of experience in media and advertising, he helps brands excel in the transition from linear TV to streaming.

His experience spans broadcast, cable, national syndication, OTT/CTV, social video, and digital activations, working with dozens of brands across major industries. Nathan also brings expertise in connecting brands with independent artists for custom music that elevates CTV and video creative.

Our Network

We partner with software engineers, web application developers, data privacy experts, and independent artists to deliver CTV strategy, technical integration, and custom music for your campaigns.
