## Overview

Combining a unique perspective of the TV advertising industry and a passion for the music industry, **Three First Names & Associates** is founded by **Nathan Michael Scott** (three first names… get it?) who aims to make a positive impact in the advertising industry by partnering with brands & agencies to fast-forward through the noise of the fragmented TV landscape and strike gold with CTV.

With nearly a decade of experience in media and advertising, Nathan is perfectly positioned to help brands excel in the transition from linear TV to streaming with extensive knowledge of video advertising spanning broadcast, cable, national syndication, OTT/CTV, social video, and digital activations as well as hands-on experience working with dozens of brands across every major industry.

In addition to an expertise in CTV, Three First Names & Associates partners with software engineers, web application developers, and data privacy experts to provide **technical data integration support** between third-party vendors and your brands' preferred in-house platforms to ensure your data science teams are focused on the projects that matter most.

But wait… it's not just CTV and technical expertise. Three First Names & Associates also connects you with a **network of independent artists** to facilitate creative collaboration for taking your video advertising campaigns to the next level with **custom music opportunities**. If you are looking to personalize your CTV ads for your target audience or trying to match the context of the program placement, one of the most effective ways to refresh the "feel" of your creatives is by customizing the music.

Whether you are seeking advice for the transition from linear to streaming, looking to optimize your CTV advertising campaigns, or require additional technical vendor & data integration support, Three First Names & Associates provides two unique offerings to enhance your CTV and video advertising strategies:

- **TFN Media Solutions** — CTV strategy, optimization, and technical integration
- **TFN Music Services** — Custom music and independent artist collaboration for video/CTV ads
